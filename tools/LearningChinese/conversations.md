# 🗣️ Flash Conversation Cards: Maker Faire Shenzhen

Cards for the flash card section of the learning Chinese website. Each card shows simplified Chinese, pinyin, and English. For yes/no questions the answer directly addresses the question.

New words used in these cards (not already in the textbook or bonus lessons 1-8) are added to `bonusvocab.md` as:

- Bonus Lesson 9: AI (人工智能)
- Bonus Lesson 10: The Event, and Sharing Our Work
- Bonus Lesson 11: Companies and Booth Visits

Textbook (chinesevocab.md) and bonus-lesson words are reused on purpose, so each card usually teaches only 1 or 2 genuinely new words. A note under each card lists which words are new.

## A. Questions people will ask me (answer to each other)

### A1: Are you a teacher?

- **Q:** 你是老师吗？ (Nǐ shì lǎoshī ma?)
  Are you a teacher?
- **A:** 对，我是老师。 (Duì, wǒ shì lǎoshī.)
  Yes, I am a teacher.

### A2: What do you teach?

- **Q:** 你教什么？ (Nǐ jiào shénme?)
  What do you teach?
- **A:** 我教设计和技术。 (Wǒ jiào shèjì hé jìshù.)
  I teach design and technology.
- New words: design / technology / teach are in bonus lesson 4. All other words are in the textbook.

### A3: Are you an English teacher?

- **Q:** 你是英语老师吗？ (Nǐ shì Yīngyǔ lǎoshī ma?)
  Are you an English teacher?
- **A:** 不，我是设计和技术老师。 (Bù, wǒ shì shèjì hé jìshù lǎoshī.)
  No, I am a design and technology teacher.
- New words: none (English is textbook, design and technology are bonus 4).

### A4: Where is your school?

- **Q:** 你的学校在哪儿？ (Nǐ de xuéxiào zài nǎr?)
  Where is your school?
- **A:** 我的学校在南山，在桃源村地铁站旁边。 (Wǒ de xuéxiào zài Nánshān, zài Táoyuáncūn dìtiě zhàn pángbiān.)
  My school is in Nanshan, next to Taoyuancun Metro station.
- New words: 南山 (Nánshān), 桃源村 (Táoyuáncūn, both place names). 学校, 在, 旁边, 地铁站 are all textbook.

### A5: What kind of things do you teach?

- **Q:** 你教什么课？ (Nǐ jiào shénme kè?)
  What classes do you teach?
- **A:** 你可以看我的网站，了解我的课程。 (Nǐ kěyǐ kàn wǒ de wǎngzhàn, liǎojiě wǒ de kèchéng.)
  You can visit my website to learn about my courses.
- New words: 了解 (to get to know), 课程 (course, curriculum). 网站 is in bonus lesson 6.

### A6: Do you offer private lessons?

- **Q:** 你教私教课吗？ (Nǐ jiào sījiào kè ma?)
  Do you offer private lessons?
- **A:** 不，我不教私教课。 (Bù, wǒ bú jiào sījiào kè.)
  No, I do not teach private lessons.
- New words: 私教课 (private lesson). All other words are textbook.

### A7: Do you have an extracurricular program?

- **Q:** 你有课外活动吗？ (Nǐ yǒu wàikè huódòng ma?)
  Do you have an extracurricular program?
- **A:** 不，我没有课外活动。 (Bù, wǒ méiyǒu wàikè huódòng.)
  No, I do not have an extracurricular program.
- New words: 课外活动 (extracurricular activity).

### A8: What is your booth about?

- **Q:** 这个展位是做什么的？ (Zhège zhànwèi shì zuò shénme de?)
  What is this booth about?
- **A:** 我分享我的项目和作品。 (Wǒ fēnxiǎng wǒ de xiàngmù hé zuòpǐn.)
  I share my projects and works.
- New words: 展位 (booth), 分享 (to share), 作品 (work, creation). 项目 is in bonus lesson 4.

### A9: Are you selling something?

- **Q:** 你在卖东西吗？ (Nǐ zài mài dōngxi ma?)
  Are you selling something?
- **A:** 我不卖东西，我的资源都是免费的。 (Wǒ bù mài dōngxi, wǒ de zīyuán dōu shì miǎnfèi de.)
  I am not selling anything, all my resources are free.
- New words: 资源 (resources), 免费 (free of charge). 卖 and 东西 are in the textbook (卖 is lesson 17).

### A10: How long have you been in China?

- **Q:** 你在中国多久了？ (Nǐ zài Zhōngguó duōjiǔ le?)
  How long have you been in China?
- **A:** 我到中国快八年了。 (Wǒ dào Zhōngguó kuài bā nián le.)
  I came to China almost eight years ago.
- New words: 快 (almost comes before a number), 多久 (how long for). 八 and 年 are in the textbook.

### A11: Can you speak Chinese?

- **Q:** 你会说汉语吗？ (Nǐ huì shuō Hànyǔ ma?)
  Can you speak Chinese?
- **A:** 我正在学汉语，说得不太好。 (Wǒ zhèngzài xué Hànyǔ, shuō de bù tài hǎo.)
  I am learning Chinese, but I do not speak it very well.
- New words: none (all are textbook).

### A12: What do you think about AI?

- **Q:** 你觉得人工智能怎么样？ (Nǐ juéde réngōng zhìnéng zěnmeyàng?)
  What do you think about AI?
- **A:** 我对人工智能的发展很感兴趣。 (Wǒ duì réngōng zhìnéng de fāzhǎn hěn gǎn xìngqù.)
  I am very interested in the development of AI.
- New words: 觉得 (to think feel), 人工智能 (AI), 发展 (development), 感兴趣 (to be interested in). 怎么样 is the textbook.

### A13: Do you use AI in your classes?

- **Q:** 你在课上用人工智能吗？ (Nǐ zài kè shàng yòng réngōng zhìnéng ma?)
  Do you use AI in your classes?
- **A:** 我用，但是我告诉学生什么时候用，为什么用。 (Wǒ yòng, dànshì wǒ gàosù xuésheng shénme shíhòu yòng, wèishénme yòng.)
  Yes, I use it, but I tell my students when it is used and why.
- New words: 告诉 (to tell), 什么时候 (when). 为什么 and 学生 are textbook (学生 is also bonus 4).

### A14: Do your students use AI in class?

- **Q:** 你的学生也用人工智能吗？ (Nǐ de xuésheng yě yòng réngōng zhìnéng ma?)
  Do your students use AI too?
- **A:** 他们有时候用，但是大多数时候不用。 (Tāmen yǒushíhòu yòng, dànshì dàduōshù shíhòu bú yòng.)
  They sometimes use it, but mostly they do not.
- New words: 有时候 (sometimes), 大多数 (the majority, most). 但是 is the textbook.

### A15: Do you use DeepSeek?

- **Q:** 你用 DeepSeek 吗？ (Nǐ yòng DeepSeek ma?)
  Do you use DeepSeek?
- **A:** 对，我用。 (Duì, wǒ yòng.)
  Yes, I use it.
- New words: 用 (to use) is the only new word. Everyone at the event will recognize the brand name "DeepSeek".

### A16: Is this 3D printed?

- **Q:** 这是3D打印的吗？ (Zhè shì 3D dǎyìn de ma?)
  Is this 3D printed?
- **A:** 对，是3D打印的。 (Duì, shì 3D dǎyìn de.)
  Yes, it is 3D printed.
- New words: none. 打印 (to print) is in bonus lesson 2. Note the 是...的 pattern, which emphasizes the source or way it was made.

### A17: Did you design this?

- **Q:** 是你设计的吗？ (Shì nǐ shèjì de ma?)
  Did you design this?
- **A:** 对，是我设计的。 (Duì, shì wǒ shèjì de.)
  Yes, I designed it.
- New words: 设计 (design) is in bonus lesson 4. Again uses the 是...的 pattern.

### A18: Where are you from?

- **Q:** 你是哪儿来的人？ (Nǐ shì nǎr lái de rén?)
  Where are you from?
- **A:** 我是美国人。 (Wǒ shì Měiguó rén.)
  I am American.
- New words: none. 哪儿 and 来 are textbook, 美国 is the textbook.

### A19: Do you like living in China?

- **Q:** 你喜欢在中国生活吗？ (Nǐ xǐhuan zài Zhōngguó shēnghuó ma?)
  Do you like living in China?
- **A:** 对，我很喜欢。 (Duì, wǒ hěn xǐhuan.)
  Yes, I really like it.
- New words: 生活 (living, life). All others are textbook.

### A20: Where do you work?

- **Q:** 你在哪儿工作？ (Nǐ zài nǎr gōngzuò?)
  Where do you work?
- **A:** 我在深圳南山国际学校工作。(Wǒ zài Shēnzhèn Nánshān guójì xuéxiào gōngzuò.)
  I work at the International School of Nanshan Shenzhen (ISNS).
- Note: ISNS's current official Chinese name is 深圳市南山外籍人员子女学校 (Shēnzhènshì Nánshān wàijí rénguán zǐnǚ xuéxiào), adopted in 2021, but most people still call it 深圳南山国际学校 or simply ISNS. New words: 深圳 (Shēnzhèn), 国际 (guójì). 工作 and 学校 are textbook.

### A21: Extra: What does your name mean?

- **Q:** 你的名字是什么意思？ (Nǐ de míngzi shì shénme yìsi?)
  What does your name mean?
- **A:** 我的名字没有什么特别的意思，是我爸爸取的。 (Wǒ de míngzi méiyǒu shénme tèbié de yìsi, shì wǒ bàba qǔ de.)
  My name does not have a special meaning, my father chose it for me.
- New words: 特别 (special), 取 (to choose, to give as a name). Adapt this answer to your real situation. 名字, 什么, 意思, 爸爸 are all in the textbook.

## B. Statements I would say

### B1: Identifying myself

- 我是设计和技术老师。(Wǒ shì shèjì hé jìshù lǎoshī.)
  I am a design and technology teacher.
- New words: none (design, technology are bonus 4).

### B2: Which grades do I teach?

- 我教高中，从9年级到12年级。(Wǒ jiào gāozhōng, cóng jiǔ niánjí dào shí'èr niánjí.)
  I teach high school, from grade 9 to grade 12.
- New words: 高中 (high school), 年级 (grade, year in school). 九, 十, 到 are textbook. 十二 is formed from 十 plus 二.

### B3: My school

- 我在深圳南山国际学校（ISNS）工作。(Wǒ zài Shēnzhèn Nánshān guójì xuéxiào (ISNS) gōngzuò.)
  I work at the International School of Nanshan Shenzhen (ISNS).
- New words: 深圳, 国际 (see A20).

### B4: I like to build computers

- 我喜欢组装电脑。(Wǒ xǐhuan zǔzhuāng diànnǎo.)
  I like to build computers.
- New words: none (组装, 电脑 are bonus lesson 1).

### B5: I built this computer for AI models

- 我组装这台电脑，用来运行人工智能模型。(Wǒ zǔzhuāng zhè tai diànnǎo, yònglái yùnxíng réngōng zhìnéng móxíng.)
  I built this computer to run AI models.
- New words: 人工智能 (AI), 运行 (to run). 用来 "to be used for" is a fixed phrase, new. 电脑 and 组装 are bonus 1.

### B6: Visit my website

- 你可以看我的网站，了解我的工作。(Nǐ kěyǐ kàn wǒ de wǎngzhàn, liǎojiě wǒ de gōngzuò.)
  You can visit my website to learn about my work.
- New words: 了解 (see A5).

### B7: I share videos on Instagram and WeChat

- 我在 Instagram 和微信上分享视频。(Wǒ zài Instagram hé Wēixìn shàng fēnxiǎng shìpín.)
  I share videos on Instagram and WeChat.
- New words: 视频 (video), 微信 (WeChat). 分享 is new (see A8).

### B8: I am studying Chinese

- 我在学汉语，还要学很多东西。(Wǒ zài xué Hànyǔ, hái yào xué hěn duō dōngxi.)
  I am studying Chinese, and I still have a lot to learn.
- New words: 很多东西 (a lot of things). 还 is the textbook.

### B9: I did not understand

- 我不懂，请再说一遍。(Wǒ bù dǒng, qǐng zài shuō yí biàn.)
  I did not understand, please say that again.
- New words: none (请再说一遍 is a textbook phrase from lesson 2).

### B10: My school is an IB school

- 我的学校是一所国际文凭学校 (IB)。(Wǒ de xuéxiào shì yì suǒ guójì wénbīng xuéxiào (IB).)
  My school is an International Baccalaureate (IB) school.
- New words: 国际文凭 (international baccalaureate), 所 (measure word for institutions). It is OK to say "IB" in English.

### B11: I wrote an online textbook

- 我写了一本设计技术的在线教材。(Wǒ xiě le yì běn shèjì jìshù de zàixiàn jiàocái.)
  I wrote an online textbook for design and technology.
- New words: 教材 (jiàocái, teaching material, textbook), 在线 (zàixiàn, online). 写 (to write) is the textbook.

### B12: I use Autodesk Fusion for 3D modeling

- 我使用 Autodesk Fusion 做3D建模。(Wǒ shǐyòng Autodesk Fusion zuò 3D jiànmó.)
  I use Autodesk Fusion for 3D modeling.
- New words: none (三维建模 is bonus lesson 3). The brand name is read in English.

### B13: My wife's family background

- 我妻子是美国人，但是她的姥姥是中国来的。(Wǒ qīzi shì Měiguó rén, dànshì tā de lǎolao shì Zhōngguó lái de.)
  My wife is American, but her grandmother is from China.
- Note: 姥姥 (lǎolao) is the grandmother on the mother's side, which I have assumed here. If she means the paternal grandmother, use 奶奶 (nǎinai) instead. New words: 妻子 (wife), 姥姥.

### B14: I use AI for writing code

- 我用人工智能写代码。(Wǒ yòng réngōng zhìnéng xiě dàimǎ.)
  I use AI to write code.
- New words: 人工智能 (AI). 写, 代码 are existing (代码 is bonus 5).

## C. Questions I will ask at booths (and the answers to be prepared for)

Each card is a question you will ask a tech company or maker. The A / B / C answers are the responses you are likely to hear, with pinyin and English, so you can recognize them live.

### C1: Is your company in Shenzhen?

- **Q:** 你的公司在深圳吗？ (Nǐ de gōngsī zài Shēnzhèn ma?)
  Is your company located in Shenzhen?
- **A (Yes):** 对，我们的公司在深圳。(Duì, wǒmen de gōngsī zài Shēnzhèn.)
  Yes, our company is in Shenzhen.
- **B (No, somewhere else):** 不，我们的公司在东莞 (or Guangzhou, Huizhou, etc.; use the actual city name). (Bù, wǒmen de gōngsī zài Dōngguǎn.)
  No, our company is in Dongguan.
- New words: 深圳 (Shenzhen), 公司 is the textbook. 东莞 (Dōngguǎn), 广州 (Guǎngzhōu), 惠州 (Huìzhōu) are place names, new.

### C2: Can I visit your company?

- **Q:** 我可以参观你们的公司吗？ (Wǒ kěyǐ cānguā nǐmen de gōngsī ma?)
  Can I visit your company?
- **A (Yes):** 可以，你什么时候有空？(Kěyǐ, nǐ shénme shíhòu yǒu kòng?)
  Yes, when are you free?
- **B (Let's talk later):** 以后我们再说吧。(Yǐhòu wǒmen zài shuō ba.)
  Let's talk about it later.
- New words: 参观 (to visit), 有空 (to be free, to have time). 以后 is the textbook.

### C3: Can I add you on WeChat?

- **Q:** 我可以加你的微信吗？(Wǒ kěyǐ jiā nǐ de Wēixìn ma?)
  Can I add you on WeChat?
- **A (Yes!):** 当然可以，这是我的名片。(Dāngrán kěyǐ, zhè shì wǒ de míngpiàn.)
  Of course, here is my business card.
- New words: 加 (to add, in the context of WeChat), 名片 (business card). 微信 is new (see B7). 当然 is the textbook.

### C4: Do you speak English?

- **Q:** 你会说英语吗？(Nǐ huì shuō Yīngyǔ ma?)
  Do you speak English?
- **A (Yes):** 我会，说得不太好。(Wǒ huì, shuō de bù tài hǎo.)
  Yes, but I do not speak it very well.
- **B (No):** 我不会，我只说中文。(Wǒ bú huì, wǒ zhǐ shuō Zhōngwén.)
  No, I only speak Chinese.
- **C (A little):** 我会说一点英语。(Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎn Yīngyǔ.)
  I speak a little English.
- New words: 中文 (Chinese language). 英语 is the textbook. 说, 会, 一点 are all textbook.

### C5: Can I buy this product?

- **Q:** 你们的产品可以在哪儿买？(Nǐmen de chǎnpǐn kěyǐ zài nǎr mǎi?)
  Can I buy this product, and where?
- **A (Yes):** 可以在我们的网站买。(Kěyǐ zài wǒmen de wǎngzhàn mǎi.)
  You can buy it on our website.
- **B (No):** 不卖。(Bù mài.)
  We do not sell it.
- **C (Not yet):** 现在还不能买，明年可以买。(Xiānzài hái bù néng mǎi, míngnián kěyǐ mǎi.)
  You cannot buy it now, but it will be available next year.
- New words: 产品 (chǎnpǐn, product), 明年 (míngnián, next year), 不能 (cannot). 买 and 现在 are the textbook, 网站 is bonus 6.

### C6: What is this machine called?

- **Q:** 这个机器叫什么名字？(Zhège jīqì jiào shénme míngzi?)
  What is the name of this machine?
- **A (Example answer):** 这是我们上个月发布的新产品。(Zhè shì wǒmen shàng gè yuè fābù de xīn chǎnpǐn.)
  This is our new product, released last month.
- New words: 机器 (machine, device), 发布 (to release, to launch), 上个月 (last month), 新 (new). 叫 and 名字 are textbook.

### C7: How much is this?

- **Q:** 这个多少钱？(Zhège duōshao qián?)
  How much is this?
- **A (A price):** 这个五百元人民币。(Zhège wǔbǎi yuán Rénmínbì.)
  This is five hundred yuan.
- **B (Not for sale):** 这个不卖，这只是样品。(Zhège bù mài, zhè zhǐshì yàngpǐn.)
  This is not for sale, it is just a sample.
- New words: 元 (yuan, the unit of currency), 人民币 (RMB), 样品 (sample). 多少钱, 五, 百 are all in the textbook.

## Study notes

- **了 (le) after a verb** marks a completed action or a change of state. E.g. 我写了 ("I did write"), 我到中国快八年了 ("I have been in China for almost eight years now").
- **在 before a verb** marks an action in progress (I am doing). E.g. 我正在学汉语.
- **是...的 pattern**: highlights who did or made it. E.g. 是我设计的 ("it was I who designed it"), 她是中国来的 ("she is from China").
- **Yes / No answers**: the answer addresses the question directly by repeating the question's verb. E.g. when you are asked 你教私教课吗? ("do you teach private lessons?"), the answer is 对，我教私教课 or 不，我不教私教课. When one side of the exchange has already established the content, a short 对 or 不 is fine on its own (this is what cards A1, A16 and A17 do); the rest is understood. This is natural spoken Chinese, not a mistake.
